Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 25, 2026

1. No Data Collection

WinGame Booster Pro is designed with privacy as a core principle. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data, including but not limited to:

  • Name, email, or contact information
  • Browsing history or app usage patterns
  • System telemetry or analytics data

2. Local Processing

All system optimization tasks are performed locally on your device. This includes process priority adjustments, memory cleanup, and power plan management. Your process list is never uploaded to any server.

3. Network Communication

Our software only accesses the internet for the following specific purposes:

  • DNS Optimization: To test and apply faster DNS settings.
  • License Verification: To verify your PRO license via Microsoft Store.

No personal identification data is sent during these operations.

4. Third-Party Services

We do not integrate any third-party advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs. Your data stays on your machine.

5. Compliance

We comply with global privacy standards, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Since we do not collect data, your right to privacy is naturally protected.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about our privacy policy, please contact us through the Microsoft Store support channel.
